**Figure 3. Search behaviour at a single frozen state.** Both planners searched from the **same** state (fingerprint `65ecc28dcdc8e0c5`), at a matched **budget**. (3A, 3B) MCTS expands 7 distinct **root actions** with 5.14 **visits** per action on average (median 2, max 24). (3C, 3D) DPW expands 24 distinct continuous root actions with mean 1.46 (median 1, max 3); only sampled actions are drawn, since a smoothed surface would imply combinations the planner never tried. Both planners leave many candidates lightly explored, but MCTS concentrates substantially more evidence on its most-preferred discrete action, whereas DPW spreads the same budget across many continuous actions.
